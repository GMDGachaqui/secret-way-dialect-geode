# Secret Way Dialect

This mod replaces words by their equivalent in the SecretWay "Dictionnary" and some others that me and my broski Floy found.

It will even correct your typing, level descriptions and IN GAME TEXT !!!!

Be aware of updates ! New Words will surely be added in future updates.

We had help from my stepdad and ChatGPT but we coded a big part by ourselves !
